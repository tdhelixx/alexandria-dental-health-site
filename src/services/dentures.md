## Types of Dentures in Alexandria VA

There are two basic types of dentures, both of which can be removable or fixed:

### Full Denture
A full denture replaces the top or bottom arch of teeth. This comprehensive solution is ideal for patients who have lost all their teeth in either the upper or lower jaw, or both.

**Benefits of Full Dentures:**
- Complete smile restoration
- Improved facial support
- Enhanced chewing ability
- Better speech clarity
- Restored confidence

### Partial Denture
An alternative to a fixed dental bridge, a partial denture can replace several consecutive teeth with the ability to be removable. Partial dentures are designed to fit around your remaining natural teeth.

**Benefits of Partial Dentures:**
- Preserve remaining healthy teeth
- Prevent teeth from shifting
- More conservative than bridges
- Removable for easy cleaning
- Cost-effective solution

## Advanced Option: Implant-Supported Dentures

[Dental implants](/dental-implants/) offer the option of an [implant-supported denture](/implant-supported-dentures/), providing the optimal replacement option for a full denture in a process known as **All-on-Four®**. Dr. Mazhari will determine if you are a candidate for implant secured dentures during your consultation.

**Advantages of Implant-Supported Dentures:**
- Superior stability and retention
- No need for denture adhesives
- Improved chewing efficiency
- Better taste and comfort
- Preserves jawbone structure
- Long-term solution

## Caring for Your Dentures

Proper denture care is essential for:
- Maintaining oral health
- Ensuring denture longevity
- Preventing odors and stains
- Keeping your mouth comfortable

**Daily Care Tips:**
- Remove and rinse after eating
- Clean thoroughly with denture cleanser
- Soak overnight in denture solution
- Handle carefully to avoid damage
- Regular dental checkups for adjustments

## Signs You May Need Dentures

Consider dentures if you experience:

- Multiple missing teeth
- Difficulty chewing or eating
- Problems speaking clearly
- Self-consciousness about your smile
- Remaining teeth are loose or painful
- Chronic dental problems

## Denture vs. Other Options

### Dentures vs. Dental Implants
- **Dentures**: More affordable, faster treatment, non-surgical
- **Implants**: Permanent, more natural feel, preserve bone

### Dentures vs. Bridges
- **Dentures**: Replace multiple teeth, removable, more cost-effective
- **Bridges**: Fixed, require healthy adjacent teeth, smaller gaps

## Adjusting to New Dentures

It's normal to experience an adjustment period:

**First Few Days:**
- Slight discomfort or soreness
- Increased saliva production
- Minor speech changes
- Different eating sensations

**Tips for Adjustment:**
- Start with soft foods
- Practice speaking aloud
- Use denture adhesive as recommended
- Be patient with the process
- Follow all post-care instructions
