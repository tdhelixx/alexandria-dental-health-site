# Dentures & Partials in Alexandria VA

Do you have missing teeth? Suffer from ill-fitting dentures? Mojgan Mazhari, DDS can help with full & partial dentures.

Dentures in Alexandria VA are dental appliances that replace missing teeth. It can be full or partial, depending on how many of your consecutive teeth are lost. Dentures have been the traditional tooth replacement option for many decades and they have experienced just as many changes through the years, as new materials and technologies have advanced the science and technique behind replacing missing teeth.

Today's dental patients enjoy a range of tooth replacement options, all of which now offer natural-looking, comfortable results that enable a normal function. Dr. Mazhari works with patients on an individual basis to help them choose the most suitable option for replacing lost teeth while meeting their oral health needs, cosmetic goals, and budget.

## Types of Dentures in Alexandria VA

There are two basic types of dentures, both of which can be removable or fixed:

- Full denture: a full denture replaces the top or bottom arch of teeth
- Partial denture: an alternative to a fixed dental bridge, a partial denture can replace several consecutive teeth with the ability to be removable

Dental implants offer the option of an implant-supported denture, providing the optimal replacement option for a full denture in a process known as All-on-Four®. Dr. Mazhari will determine if you are a candidate for implant secured dentures during your consultation.

### Dentures: What To Expect

Dr. Mazhari will meet with you to evaluate your oral health and discuss your concerns when it comes to the aesthetics and function of your smile. Our goal is to provide a treatment solution that will maintain your long-term dental health while also providing you with a natural-looking and comfortable smile that boosts your confidence. We want your smile to support your best quality of life each day, allowing you to enjoy a healthy diet and active social life.

Your procedure will be enhanced by the use of advanced digital technology and the highest quality materials for a final restoration that blends with your natural appearance and fits securely in your mouth. Whenever possible, Dr. Mazhari will recommend the use of an implant secured denture for the most stable result that does not require daily adhesives.

Dr. Mazhari will explain how to care for your dentures to ensure that they will last and keep your smile healthy. If your dentures are removable, we will offer tips on how to keep them clean and when to come in for periodic adjustments so they remain comfortable and secure.
