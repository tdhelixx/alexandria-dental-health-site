## What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. They consist of a titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root, supporting a custom-made crown that looks and functions just like your natural teeth.

Unlike removable dentures or bridges that rely on adjacent teeth for support, dental implants are standalone solutions that preserve your jawbone and don't affect neighboring healthy teeth.

## Why Choose Dental Implants?

### Superior to Other Options

**Compared to Dentures:**
- No slipping or clicking
- Eat all your favorite foods
- No adhesives needed
- More comfortable fit

**Compared to Bridges:**
- Don't affect adjacent teeth
- Easier to clean and maintain
- Last longer with proper care
- Preserve jawbone structure

### Long-Term Investment

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. They're designed to integrate with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration, creating a permanent foundation for your replacement tooth.

## Are You a Candidate?

Good candidates for dental implants typically have:

- Sufficient jawbone density
- Healthy gums
- Good overall health
- Commitment to oral hygiene
- Non-smoker (or willing to quit)

During your consultation, Dr. Mazhari will evaluate your specific situation and discuss whether dental implants are right for you.

## Caring for Your Implants

Dental implants require the same care as natural teeth:

- Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
- Floss daily around the implant crown
- Regular dental checkups and cleanings
- Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol
