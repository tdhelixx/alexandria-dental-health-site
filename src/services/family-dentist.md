## When Should Children First Visit the Dentist?

The American Dental Association recommends that children have their first dental visit by age one or within six months of their first tooth appearing. Early visits help:

- Establish a dental home
- Monitor proper tooth development
- Identify potential problems early
- Create positive associations with dental care
- Educate parents on proper oral care

## Family Dental Health Tips

### Daily Oral Care
- Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
- Floss daily once teeth are touching
- Use age-appropriate oral care products
- Supervise children's brushing until age 8-10

### Healthy Habits
- Limit sugary snacks and drinks
- Choose tooth-healthy foods
- Don't share utensils with babies
- Replace toothbrushes every 3-4 months

### Regular Dental Visits
- Schedule checkups every six months
- Address dental concerns promptly
- Maintain consistency with the same dental team
- Keep dental appointments even if teeth "look fine"

## Making Dental Visits Fun for Kids

We employ several strategies to ensure children feel comfortable and safe:

**Before the Visit:**
- Talk positively about the dentist
- Read dental-themed books together
- Practice opening wide and counting teeth
- Avoid using dental visits as threats or punishments

**During the Visit:**
- Allow children to see and touch dental instruments
- Use positive, non-threatening language
- Celebrate good behavior and cooperation
- Take breaks if needed for comfort

**After the Visit:**
- Praise children for their cooperation
- Follow any specific care instructions
- Schedule the next visit before leaving
- Reinforce positive messages about dental health
