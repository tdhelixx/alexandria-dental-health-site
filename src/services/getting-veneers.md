# Getting Veneers at Alexandria Dental Health & Smile Studio

A smile can make all the difference in your confidence and appearance. At Alexandria Dental Health & Smile Studio in Alexandria, VA Dr. Mojgan Mazhari has over 15 years of experience to help you get the smile of your dreams with veneers. Our practice specializes in advanced ceramic technologies that address structural compromise, color variations, and morphological irregularities in dental surfaces.

### Our Services - What we offer

Our veneer service at Alexandria Dental Health & Smile Studio is designed to meet your unique needs. Here are the different services offered by us:

- Porcelain veneers are durable, natural-looking, and custom-made to match your desired smile aesthetic.
- Lumineers are minimally invasive veneers requiring little to no enamel removal.
- Smile Makeovers are complete treatment plans that combine veneers with other cosmetic procedures.

Our facility employs CEREC Primescan technology, integrating digital scanning with computer-aided design and manufacturing. This system enables precise margin adaptation and optimal contour development for each restoration.

### Treatment Philosophy

Our approach centers on evidence-based ceramic restoration techniques that preserve natural tooth structure. We prioritize long-term functional success through careful material selection and precise application protocols. This methodology incorporates patient education, detailed treatment planning, and implementation of current dental material innovations.

### Understanding Custom Dental Veneers

At Alexandria Dental Health & Smile Studio, we provide personalized care to make sure your veneers are custom-designed for you. Here’s how we can help you get your dream smile:

What Are Veneers?

- Thin, custom-made shells that cover the front of your teeth.
- Perfect for correcting discoloration, gaps, chips, and uneven shapes.
- Made from high-quality porcelain that mimics the natural look of enamel.

Veneer Process

- Dr. Mazhari listens to your goals and evaluates your oral health.
- Digital imaging ensures precise design and a seamless, comfortable fit.
- Each veneer is custom-crafted to match your aesthetic preferences and functional needs.

Why Choose Veneers?

- Achieve a radiant, flawless smile you’ll be proud to share.
- With proper care, veneers can last 10 to 15 years or more.
- Veneers require minimal alteration of natural teeth.

Your Experience Matters

- Enjoy a stress-free process in our welcoming Alexandria office.
- We create treatment options according to your unique smile.
- Veneers blend seamlessly for a lifelike appearance.
