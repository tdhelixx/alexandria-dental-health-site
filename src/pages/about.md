# About Alexandria Dental Health

## Meet Dr. Mojgan Mazhari

Dr. Mojgan Mazhari has been providing exceptional dental care to the Alexandria, Virginia community for over 20 years. With a commitment to excellence and patient comfort, Dr. Mazhari and her experienced team offer comprehensive dental services using the latest technology and techniques.

### Education & Training
- Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from Virginia Commonwealth University
- Advanced training in cosmetic and restorative dentistry
- Continuing education in dental implants and modern dental techniques

### Our Practice Philosophy
At Alexandria Dental Health, we believe that every patient deserves personalized, gentle care in a comfortable environment. We take the time to listen to your concerns and develop treatment plans tailored to your unique needs and goals.

## Our Team
Our experienced dental team is dedicated to providing exceptional care and ensuring your comfort throughout every visit. From our skilled dental hygienists to our friendly front office staff, everyone at Alexandria Dental Health is committed to your oral health and satisfaction.

## Modern Technology
We use state-of-the-art technology to provide the most effective and comfortable dental treatments:
- Digital X-rays for reduced radiation exposure
- CEREC same-day crowns
- Advanced sterilization systems
- Comfortable sedation options
