## Our Services

- **Dental Whitening:** Our treatments give you this brightness and shine on your whitening as they peel off stains and provide a more youthful appearance
- **Dental bonding:** Same-day repair of chips and gaps using color-matched composite resin. Durable, natural-looking results.
- **Veneers:** Digital-designed porcelain or composite veneers. Precise color matching, fit, and bite alignment.
- **Smile Makeovers:** Full treatment plans using 3D imaging and bite analysis. Combines multiple procedures for optimal results.
- **Invisalign:** Clear aligners with digital tracking. Regular 3D scan monitoring for precise tooth movement.

Dr. Mazhari has specialized neuromuscular and cosmetic dentistry training from the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies. This expertise allows for comprehensive treatment addressing both aesthetics and oral function. Named Top Dentist by Northern Virginia Magazine 2013-2018.
