## New Patient Information

Your first dental visit with Dr. Mazhari in our Alexandria dentist's office provides the opportunity for us to get to know each other. We hope that this initial visit is the beginning of a lasting doctor-patient relationship that will support not only your dental health, but your overall physical well-being. Dr. Mazhari considers herself a partner in your healthcare journey, and she will take the time during your visit to understand your unique oral health concerns and address any goals that you may have when it comes to the appearance of your natural smile.

Our objective as your dental provider is to deliver the best possible dental care in a warm, supportive environment so that you can enjoy the lasting benefits of good oral health and a stunning smile.

## Your First Appointment in Alexandria VA

Dr. Mazhari welcomes new patients, and she will take a thorough and comprehensive approach to your dental care. During your first visit, you can expect to be in our office for about **90 minutes**, during which you will receive:

- A consultation and dental exam with Dr. Mazhari
- A periodontal screening
- A cavity screening
- Diagnostic x-rays, as needed

Depending on your specific needs or concerns, we may also perform a dental cleaning during your initial visit. Following your consultation and exam, Dr. Mazhari can provide treatment recommendations for addressing health or cosmetic concerns. We want you to understand and enjoy the many benefits that good oral health can have on your overall health and quality of life.

## What to Bring to Your First Visit

We know how busy our patients are, and we try to respect your time when you visit us. That is why we have tried to streamline the check-in process as much as possible. Please bring the following items with you to your appointment:

- Your dental insurance card and information, if applicable
- Your completed patient forms, which are conveniently available for download in the next section of this page
- Your smile!

If you have questions or concerns about any of the forms, please bring them with you or contact us before your visit and we will assist you.
