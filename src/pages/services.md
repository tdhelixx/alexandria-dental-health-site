# Our Dental Services

At Alexandria Dental Health, we provide comprehensive dental care using the latest technology and techniques. Dr. Mojgan Mazhari and our experienced team are dedicated to helping you achieve optimal oral health and the smile you've always wanted.

## Cosmetic Dentistry Services

### [Dental Implants](/dental-implants/)
Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement solution that preserves your jawbone and doesn't affect adjacent teeth.

### [Veneers](/veneers/)
Thin porcelain shells that create beautiful, natural-looking smiles by covering imperfections.

### [Teeth Whitening](/teeth-whitening/)
Professional whitening treatments to brighten your smile safely and effectively.

### [Dental Crowns](/dental-crowns/)
Custom-made crowns that restore damaged teeth while providing natural appearance and function.

## Orthodontic Services

### [Invisalign](/invisalign/)
Clear aligners that straighten teeth discreetly and comfortably without traditional metal braces.

## Restorative Services

### [Root Canal Therapy](/root-canal-therapy/)
Pain-free treatment to save damaged or infected teeth and preserve your natural smile.

### [Dental Bridges](/dental-bridges/)
Replace missing teeth with custom-made bridges that restore function and appearance.

### [Dentures](/dentures/)
Complete and partial dentures to replace missing teeth and restore your confidence.
